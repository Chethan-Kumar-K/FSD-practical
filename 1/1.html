Three changes that would cut the most

  1. 1

    Take the metro or a bus for three car trips a week

    Transport is your largest source. Trading 12 km car trips for the metro saves the most for the least change.

    −20 kgper month

  2. 2

    Set the air conditioner to 24–26 °C

    A few degrees higher cuts electricity use, and electricity is about 30% of your month.

    −4 kgper month

  3. 3

    Swap four chicken meals for vegetarian ones

    Each swap saves about 0.9 kg, so four a month adds up without changing how you eat the rest of the time.

    −3.6 kgper month

Together: about 27.6 kg a month, 15% of your footprint. Sample estimates.
